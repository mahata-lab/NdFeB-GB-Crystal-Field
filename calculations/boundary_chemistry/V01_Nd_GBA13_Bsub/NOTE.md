directly from the step-1 orbitals with larger level shifts) converge (||g|| = 9e-4) to an active space of 6 f + 1 s orbitals
(4f/6s mixing, as for Ce at the 4f-parent sites), which AILFT cannot use. The same substitution is reported for Dy (V05) and
at class B (V03, V07). The inputs here are the production inputs as generated by embedding/build_inputs.py.
