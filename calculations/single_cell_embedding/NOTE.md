# Archived finite-cell production inputs

These are the original separate production calculations: one quantum Ln(III) ion in 67 bulk or 135 interface point charges, with RE +3, Fe 0 and B -3. The embedding charge sums are +9 e and +21 e. A charge sum alone does not establish validity or invalidity of the local potential.

The current main tables and Figures 2-4 use the saved magnetic-property reruns in `../finite_cell_magnetic/`, labeled `FINITE_*`. These production inputs correspond to the separate `PUB_*` results. Small differences are documented in `analysis/tables/finite_cell_run_reconciliation.csv`; do not combine the two labels as independent samples. Historical `PUB_Gd_*` records are aliases of the saved Gd magnetic-property outputs.

The finite-cell contrast is bulk atom 2 (4g) versus interface atom 13 (4f parent); it mixes parent site and interface effects. Sphere/matched-site sensitivity checks do not preserve all original trends. No model is declared uniquely accurate here. The point-charge files are unchanged.
